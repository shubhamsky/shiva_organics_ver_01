Our Heritage & Promise

About Shivora Organics

Born out of a commitment to bring honest, unadulterated nourishment back to Indian kitchens, Shivora Organics operates its own wooden cold-press oil mill in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra.

Every batch begins with sun-dried, farmer-sourced seeds and indigenous A2 cow milk. We never rush extraction—allowing the wooden press to turn slowly so natural enzymes, aroma, and heart-healthy fatty acids stay intact in every drop.
